How payment works

  1. Owner enters the MTN/Airtel recipient number.
  2. Student sees the number on payment.html.
  3. Student sends the required amount.
  4. Student enters the transaction code.
  5. Payment appears as Pending.
  6. Admin/Owner checks the transaction and approves it.
  7. Quiz access becomes available.
